WELCOME TO VOICEINK (free build)
================================

Voice-to-text for your Mac. 100% private: your voice never leaves
your computer.

HOW TO INSTALL (2 steps)
------------------------
1. Drag "VoiceInk" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.
2. Open your Applications folder, RIGHT-CLICK VoiceInk and choose
   "Open", then click "Open" again in the dialog.

   Why right-click? This free build isn't registered with Apple
   (that costs $99/year), so macOS shows a warning the FIRST time
   only. Right-click -> Open tells your Mac you trust it. After
   this one time, it opens normally forever.

FIRST LAUNCH
------------
The app walks you through everything:
- Allow the Microphone (so it can hear you)
- Allow Accessibility (so it can type the text for you)
- The recommended AI model downloads automatically (~600 MB, one time)

Then just press the shortcut, speak, and your words appear wherever
your cursor is. Works in every app, even without internet.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
- macOS 14.4 or newer, Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) recommended.
